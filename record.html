Sensor test recorder

Records the phone’s motion sensors while a voice guides you, then saves a CSV file. Nothing is stored on the phone or sent anywhere unless you share the file.

Safety first

  • Only small, controlled slips. Never a real fall.
  • Hold a rail, a counter or a helper’s hand during every slip.
  • Use a smooth floor in socks, with nothing hard around you.
  • Stop at any time: tap Stop or just take the phone out.

Plan

  1. Put the phone in your right sock, upright against your shin, screen out (same as the app).
  2. Stand still for 5 seconds.
  3. Walk normally (around the room or a hallway).
  4. Slip trials: the voice counts “3, 2, 1, slip now”. Do one small slip, then keep walking.
